Formula, thresholds & evidence status

BRI equation: 100 × (0.50·SSOH + 0.25·SRUL + 0.25·SRi).

Safety gate: SOH < 80% OR Ri > 0.150 Ω means BRI = 0 and REJECTED, regardless of the other scores. Equality at SOH = 80% and Ri = 0.150 Ω passes these two specific checks.

  • BRI ≥ 70: High-demand reuse (e.g. stationary storage).
  • 40 ≤ BRI < 70: Light-duty reuse (lower power / cycling duty).
  • BRI < 40: Recycle / low grade, including every rejected cell.

Evidence status: The SOH and Ri safety gate and BRI boundaries are configured decision rules. The 0.150 Ω resistance reference is provisional pending evaluation against the full dataset. Specific application assignments are proposed recommendations, not experimentally validated outcomes.
